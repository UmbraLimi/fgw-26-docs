## Learnings
- **Voice / jargon.** Operator's standing guidance for this site's copy: do **not** dumb everything down to
  the lay reader — retain a *light sampling* (one or two) of expected technical terms as a competence signal
  ("user stories", "context limits"), and plain-language the rest. A guiding principle for the
  `[SITE-CONSISTENCY]` sweep, not just this conv.
- **Lead-then-cards pattern.** A card row reads better when a short eyebrow→`h2`→sentence lead poses what the
  cards answer (the "Find your phase" band already did this; the Judgment and "What I do" bands were missing
  it). Card titles should be real `h3` headings under the section `h2`, never sentence-long "eyebrows".
- **Confidentiality reflex.** A request to name a person in public copy ("Gabriel") had to be checked against
  `[CASE-RECORD-SIGNOFF]` before acting — naming him links a confidential engagement to the publicly-named
  Urantia cases. Surfaced and deferred rather than done.
