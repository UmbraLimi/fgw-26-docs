## Learnings
- GSC request-indexing is reliably **~1-day effective** on this site (Conv-44 push verified). **Live-test-FIRST
  then Request-Index** avoids the Server-connection flake. New/changed pages need an **explicit sitemap
  resubmit + request-index** — GSC's sitemap last-read lags days; don't assume timely auto-discovery.
- **LinkedIn distributes for weeks** (impressions tail to ~day 30); **X is ~done in a day** (half-life ~80 min)
  → wall-clock horizons per channel, not conv-count.
- LinkedIn **bakes a post's link-preview at share time**; fixing og:title won't refresh an already-published
  post's card — helps future shares only.
- **Process scar:** don't write load-bearing specifics into a durable doc as "decided" (and never attribute
  CC's own choice to the operator) before the operator rules — propose, then enshrine.
- The Chrome `resize_window` didn't actually shrink the viewport here — couldn't force a <768 render; the
  operator verified the breakpoint visually instead.
