hopper item, the main thread opened on the operator's ruling that **X does a different job than LinkedIn —
not an echo**. We gave X its own content model (originate from the 40-years **Judgement thread**) and its own

is ephemeral, the lasting skill is the **judgement to know when to switch**, then learn the new tool fast;

low-risk), and the colophon engravings stay a **site** carrier — a true-scale X mock showed the ornate plate
overshadows the post text and reads "why a shell?" without on-page context. British/Canadian spelling set as
a standing rule.

## Learnings
- **Process win — the `.scratch` copy-from + chat-redline loop.** A dozen small operator edits (wording,
  emoji, line break, spelling) landed cleanly through one readable draft file; the operator sees + copies,
  CC edits. Fast and friction-free for iterative post polishing.
- **A cheap true-scale mock beats a design debate.** The engraving-on-X question was settled in one render:
  seeing the ornate plate at real X size turned a "nice idea" into a clear no (overshadows + confuses). Mock
  the real thing rather than argue it.
- **Revisit flagged caveats with evidence.** The whale-emoji caution was overstated from memory; a quick
  search (Fail Whale retired 2013, a specific illustration not the emoji; crypto "whale" is finance-niche)
  reversed it. Research the caveat before it hardens into a rule.
