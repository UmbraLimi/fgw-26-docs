## Learnings
- **Hand-coloured plates duotone cleanly.** The Bloch pike (the set's first hand-coloured plate) desaturated
  to the warm duotone and read as one family with the Trust/Steering engravings — `-colorspace Gray` before
  `+level-colors` does it. And **cropping tight to the page before duotoning fixes the level stretch**: the
  dark book-spread pixels otherwise skew `-level`, muddying the result. A book-spread scan is a fine
  *source*; the crop is the cleanup (operator's instinct, confirmed on-screen).
- **A format earns codification at its third instance.** Trust (reverse-engineered) + Steering
  (reconstructed ad hoc) were enough to see the dossier shape; baking it into step 17 *before* Deciding
  graduated meant Deciding's dossier was written to spec, not reinvented — the "pipeline is the product"
  discipline paying for itself.
- **Verify the plate, not the title.** Criterion 4 held: the Bloch scan was downloaded and *viewed* before
  use (it was the pike), and the page was cropped on measured pixel bounds, not guesses.
