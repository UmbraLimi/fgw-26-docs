## Learnings
- **FAQ-lifted headings must be self-contained.** The guide's `?`-H2s auto-lift into FAQPage JSON-LD
  (`DEC-043-3`), so they're consumed standalone in search/GEO where a bare "it" has no antecedent. Seat the
  noun in the ones that genuinely dangle (H1, H4) rather than every one (avoids "AI" in all five headings).
- **`description` vs `snippet` routing.** On guide detail pages, `description` renders as the on-page lede +
  the hub card; the SERP/og/twitter meta is `snippet ?? description`. Punchier card voice does **not** reach
  search unless the `snippet` is edited.
- **Confidence is a non-signal for AI correctness.** The AI is wrong "in the same sure, fluent tone it uses
  when it's right" — so "check when it sounds most sure" is useless; the discriminating tell is the reader's
  **intuition** (earned domain knowledge), acted on by *asking the AI*, whose record lets it name what was
  only sensed.
- **"intuition" is a sanctioned throughline word** (VOICE/PERSONA, Conv 040), not one to dodge — Steering
  had zero literal uses before this conv; naming it at the confident-nonsense beat ties Steering to the
  Trust-guide throughline. The repetition risk is in conversation, not the prose.
- **`.scratch/` lives in `fgw-26-docs`** (its gitignored home), not the website repo — regenerate the
  coordinate view to the docs-repo path.
