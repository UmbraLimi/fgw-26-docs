## Learnings
- **An og:image doesn't live on the page.** The share card is the meta tag + a `public/` asset, independent
  of on-page content. Setting it per-page keeps the LinkedIn click-card *and* brands it; attaching an image
  to the post instead suppresses the auto card. Putting the same art on the page is a separate (nice) choice.
- **Theme-aware label contrast flips with the fill.** White-on-fill reads fine in light mode but fails WCAG
  AA on the *lighter* dark-theme pillar fills (~2.85:1 on teal). A theme-aware knockout (`--surface`: near-white
  light, near-black dark) passes both. Compute the contrast; don't assume a label colour that works in one theme works in the other.
- **"I'll handle it at /r-end" is a no-op promise.** The close distills what's already captured durably; a
  prose promise routes nowhere. Capture pending DECs in the hopper as they're made — now backstopped by a hook trigger.
