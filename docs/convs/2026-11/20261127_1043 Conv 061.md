## Learnings
- **Process / lifecycle / friction.** "Pending DEC to distill" reminders parked in the hopper fight `/r-end`'s
  Step 0b gate (any open `- [ ]` halts) and are redundant with Step 2's distill. Trust Step 2 to distill from
  the conv's work; don't stash DEC reminders in the hopper.
- **Tooling / data model / staleness.** A qualitative `read:` line that embeds numbers goes stale the instant
  the numbers change — keep summary prose **number-free** when the numbers live in structured fields beside it.
- **Content strategy / POSSE.** Decouple "does a post carry an outbound link" from "is it connected to our
  content": link-less social for reach + an on-site canonical home that references the post is a coherent funnel.
