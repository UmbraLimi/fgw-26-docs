## Learnings
- **Verify in the running product beat reasoning from a label.** I'd theorized the old trailing-slash paths
  (`/services/` etc.) were serving 200 and getting indexed; a live `curl` showed they 301 on both forms — the
  GSC "indexed" entries were just stale snapshots. The dashboard label was not the ground truth.
- **An unverified claim stated as fact got caught.** Early in the read I asserted "no links/CTA in the close,
  as expected" without checking — the source did carry the CTA. Label claims honestly (the CLAUDE.md rule);
  "as expected" is not "I checked."
- **GSC "discovered" ≠ "crawled / categorized."** Sitemap submission guarantees discovery (22 shown), not
  indexing; the Page-indexing report only counts URLs Google has crawled and decided, so a 22-vs-17 gap is
  the discovery backlog — expected for a new, low-authority site, not a misconfiguration.
- **The invisible link was a contrast problem, not a missing underline.** On the deep warm paper no
  AA-legible ember is also distinct from near-black ink, so colour alone can't fix it — a second signal
  (weight) is what makes a no-underline link read as a link.
