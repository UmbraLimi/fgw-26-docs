## Progress
This conv turned `[POSTING-PLAN]` from a to-do into a standing strategy. The core artefact is
`reference/CHANNELS.md` — a third content axis (channel/funnel) beside PERSONA (who) and VOICE (sound). The
load-bearing insight that shaped it: **word of mouth is the real, unpredictable top of funnel**, so the
digital channels are mostly a confirmation/qualification layer — and the cultivated *second* entry is
**inbound discovery** (search + GEO), which makes the site's evergreen content an acquisition asset and so
makes a **content home** (guides + blog) load-bearing. Capacity (1–2 multi-month projects) drove the modest
posture throughout: biweekly LinkedIn, opportunistic X, no Premium, quality over volume.

Mid-conv, a **misreading of `DEC-024-1`** sent us down a "who edits files" detour (a marketing-doc carve-out)
before the operator caught it and reaffirmed the real model: **CC is sole editor of every file; the operator
directs or approves and never edits** — now explicitly per-project. The rest was execution: the X pinned post
(operator-led Straw Man copy, several redlines, em-dash + fragment conventions born along the way), then two
rounds on the **OG share card** — first to fix that it named no *subject*, then to augment it (credential,
experience, "steered" tagline, headshot) — each prod-deployed and mirrored onto the X pin.

## Learnings
- **A project rule written in CC's first person can be misread.** `DEC-024-1` ("I am the sole editor…") uses
  "I" = Claude; acting before confirming the document's voice inverted it and spawned a needless carve-out.
  Verify the voice first. (Mitigated: the rewritten CLAUDE.md bullet now says "I (Claude)".)
- **X renders no link-preview card for external links** — a linked post shows as bare text + link; attach an
  image for a visual (there's no card to suppress). **And X can't swap media on a posted tweet** — updating a
  pin's image means delete + repost + re-pin. (Both noted in `CHANNELS.md`.)
