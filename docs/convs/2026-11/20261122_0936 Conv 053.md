## Learnings
- **Render in the real product — figures lie in source.** The matrix only exposed its problems (serif font
  reading as prose, muted dark panel, unnamed axes, an unexplained "it") once rendered in both themes in
  Chrome, never from the markup. Screenshot-and-look drove every improvement.
- **A standalone graphic must carry its own referents.** Named axes + a scenario line were required because
  the figure doubles as a shareable artifact; in-context clarity isn't enough once it's lifted out of the page.
