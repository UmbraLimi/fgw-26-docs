### Content-process extended — coordinate view + point-form round-trip (`DEC-050-2`)
- Added two `GUIDE-CREATE.md` steps after the first draft: **step 5 — coordinate view** (`.scratch/<guide>-view.txt`,
  `number-content.mjs`; `*-view.txt` convention) and **step 6 — point-form skeleton + augmentation round-trip**
  (`.scratch/<guide>-points.txt`, `*-points.txt`): CC boils each paragraph to terse bullets (WHAT) + a kept WHY,
  **mirroring the draft, inventing nothing**; CC's own suggestions/questions go **only in `[square brackets]`**;
  the operator augments with the missing bits (unbracketed) and keeps/cuts the bracketed ones; CC then writes the
  **second draft** from the result. The `r-content` skill picks these up (it reads `GUIDE-CREATE`). Handoff + skill
  summary + `[GUIDE-DECIDING]` row updated.
- Origin: the operator caught the first point-form importing the guide's *later* model into paragraphs that didn't
  state it (P2's "too much"/"too confusing" vs the draft's understanding × risk test). The division of labour —
  CC distils (flow, cause/effect), the operator supplies the experience-side bits (incl. long-tail associations) —
  keeps the final voice his, not an imitation.
- **Deciding** is the first piece through it: generated `deciding-view.txt` (28 blocks) + a faithful
  `deciding-points.txt` (rewritten to the operator's bullet format, his P2 verbatim, one `[CC Q]` on the P2 capacity
