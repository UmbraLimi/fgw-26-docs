## Learnings
- **A fill colour validated at 3:1 (graphic) is not AA as text.** The pillar fills passed the dataviz
  contrast check (≥3:1 on surface) but failed 4.5:1 as the eyebrow label on the warm paper — the site already
  solved this with `--accent`/`--accent-strong`; per-pillar colour needs the same split. Compute text
  contrast separately, never assume the mark tone carries to text.
- **Categorical colour has a hard ceiling (~8 CVD-safe hues).** "Muted toward brand" collides with
  "distinguishable" fast; 10 muted hues failed the validator outright. Where every use-site also shows a
  label, colour past the ceiling is fine — it stops being the sole identity channel.
