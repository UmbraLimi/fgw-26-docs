## Learnings
- **A/B prose comparison is a real decision tool.** Asked whether naming the AI-bound "it"s would read
  better, drafting *both* versions and reading them — with two signals (does it repeat "the AI… the AI"? does
  the alternative leave a software-ambiguous "it"?) — showed the answer was per-sentence, not blanket: two of
  four won, two kept "it". Draft-and-read beats arguing in the abstract on close prose calls.
- **The voice caps are guidelines in service of balance, not mechanical limits.** The operator's conjunction
  and metaphor caps exist to keep the reader feeling talked to directly; the distinctive/unexpected phrasings
  are character, not clutter, and a cut is never about provenance (a CC-written line can be the keeper).
