## Learnings
- **A feature prompted by one case belonged to the whole set.** The "why checkable" framing surfaced while
  reviewing `this-site`, but the right home was every audited case — single-sourced in one component, not
  authored per case. Reviewing case-by-case still surfaced a cross-cutting need; the fix was cross-cutting.
- **A guard can be wrong by being too eager.** The A/B hook was green and "working," but it was enforcing a
  stricter rule than the one intended — blocking legitimate open-ended questions. The operator's correction,
  not the hook's success, defined the real rule. (A sibling-project echo: a guard that fires where it
  shouldn't is as much a defect as one that never fires.)
- **The checkable-work ethos reaches the case body, not just the audit.** `learning-marketplace`'s "hundred
  thousand lines" was true but uncited in the linked record; on a site whose pitch is "check my work," a
  headline figure the record doesn't carry is worth reconciling.
