## Learnings
- **Verify a premise against its consumer.** The hopper said "post `stats:` stub with `status: active` at
  creation"; the consumer (`/r-post-stats`) skips non-published posts, so a draft needs only a placeholder and
  stats fill at publish. The written premise was wrong until checked against what reads it.
- **The Stop hook only sees the turn's final message.** A no-op promise written in an *intermediate* message
  block (before more tool calls) never reaches `check-promises`. Combined with `/r-end` being habitually
  backticked, a backtick-tolerant regex just adds descriptive-mention noise. Net: the hook stays bare-match;
  operator review + discipline is the real guard, not the hook.
