## Learnings
- **Context: the de-identification guard earned its keep. / Learning:** a client's first name reached the
  site source only through a *working-folder name* that surfaced in a subagent's read — caught by a scan,
  not by reading the prose. **Pattern:** scan the rendered artifact (and agent output) for identifiers,
  not just the copy you wrote; de-identification is a scan step, not a writing intention.
- **Context: the "The record" feature. / Learning:** an AI assessing the person who will publish the
  result is not independent; what makes it credible is the *cited, checkable record*, not the AI's
  authority — and that only holds literally for owned/public repos. **Pattern:** when a claim's warrant is
  "you can check it," ship the citations, and be honest where the reader actually can't.
- **Context: the visuals read "AI-default."** A warm palette decided in tokens (`DEC-004-6`) did not
  survive implementation — low-chroma neutrals + an accent used on ~2 buttons + zero imagery read as
  monochrome boilerplate. **Pattern:** a design intent isn't delivered until it's verified in the live
  render, not just set in tokens (a sibling of "green ≠ proven").
