## Learnings

- **Context:** The sibling projects show a documentation-rigor method that repeatedly over-grew.
  **Learning:** rigor is a cost that competes with the product; start lean and grow only on demonstrated
  need. **Pattern:** adopt the *shape* of a proven system, not its accreted mass.
- **Context:** The most impressive builds are client-owned and name real clients throughout.
  **Learning:** the site's asset is the de-identified *narrative + method*, not public links to client
  code. **Pattern:** publishability is a first-class filter on evidence, decided before writing copy.
