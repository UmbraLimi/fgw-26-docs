## Progress
The conv was triggered by the Conv-001 A/B guard failing in front of the operator — it neither caught a
malformed `/r-start` turn nor passed a correctly-formatted one. Investigation (live transcript) showed
the `tail -n1` read the wrong record because CC flushes the transcript asynchronously. A
`claude-code-guide` subagent confirmed from the CC 2.1.310 docs that the transcript lag is documented,
that `last_assistant_message` is the race-free Stop-hook field, and that **no pre-render/display hook
can block or regenerate a turn** (`MessageDisplay` is display-only) — so this guard is inherently
after-the-fact, a ceiling the operator explicitly accepted. The operator then tightened the design
twice: scope the check to the ask block, and target the *last* anchor so a `👉👉👉` merely mentioned in
prose can't mis-target it. The guard is now live-verified and backed by a re-runnable suite.

## Decisions
- DEC-002-1 — A/B Stop guard reads `last_assistant_message` (not the racy transcript) and assesses only
  the ask block after the last `👉👉👉`; DEC-001-1's decision stands, only the mechanism changed.

## Learnings
- **Live verification caught what green tests could not (`DEC-000-7` in action).** A 13-case suite was
  green, yet a live debug-dump of the *extracted block* exposed a first-vs-last-anchor bug (my own turn
  had mentioned `👉👉👉` in prose). When a hook's uncertain part is the shape of its real input, keep a
  temporary dump and read it from a live stop before trusting the logic.
- **The guard can only nag, never prevent.** No CC hook sees assistant prose before it is displayed;
  the Stop hook forces a corrective re-ask after the fact. Recorded in `DEC-002-1`.
