## Learnings
- **A "reference" repo can silently go stale.** `frasergorrie` was 2+ years behind the live site; mining it
  produced a false conclusion ("no FAQ exists"). Check a reference's last-commit date against the live site
  before trusting it. Captured durably in `_cc/repo-layout.md` + `DEC-013-3`.
- **Auto-mode classifier blocks persistence edits (e.g. `~/.zshrc`).** The clean path is the `!<command>`
  route the operator runs in-session, not steering them to disable auto mode. Now a CLAUDE.md rule.
- **Behavioral (operator-flagged):** I gated a standing-right action (`/r-commit`) and an already-agreed next
  step behind a yes/no, and over-offered "disable auto mode". Act on standing rights + agreed next steps
  without seeking a nod; reach for the safer non-mode-toggling route first.
