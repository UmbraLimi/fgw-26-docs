## Learnings
- **The capture hook sweeps non-operator prompts too.** Subagent hand-backs and system
  task-notifications arrive as user-role prompts and land in `.conv-turns.jsonl`; the dialogue
  companion must filter to genuine operator turns (done here via a `jq` exclude). Pattern — may inform
  a future `capture-turn.sh` refinement, not yet an always-on rule.
- **Nested single quotes don't survive the skill `!`-command wrapping.** An embedded `awk '…'` had its
  `$1`/`$2` exposed to a shell. Named vars + `read` (no inner quotes) is the robust idiom for precompute
  commands.
