## Learnings
- **A fuller artifact leaks more than its summary.** The de-identified client *summaries* were safe, but the
  full audit *transcripts* carry far more identifying detail — and the audit itself revealed the confidential
  client is the same entity named publicly elsewhere on the site. Rendering the richer artifact publicly
  demanded a redaction pass the summary never needed. Pattern: re-examine de-identification whenever the
  surface area of what's shown grows.
- **Subagent hand-backs land in `.conv-turns.jsonl` as user-role turns.** The dialogue companion had to
  filter them (and the mid-turn system notifications) out to isolate the operator's half. Minor capture-hook
  wrinkle, noted here; not worth a fix this conv.
