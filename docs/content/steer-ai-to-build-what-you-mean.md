## 1. Take
Planning matters, but getting AI to build what you **meant** takes continuous **steering** with the context
only you have — the things in your head. The piece names the gap precisely: it is the **transmission** gap
(intent you hold but never voiced; the AI can't read your mind), **not** the discovery/planning gap (what
you'll actually use and can't pre-specify even to yourself — that is a separate future **Planning** guide,
`DEC-044-1`). Steering is framed as a learnable skill ("becoming a worthy companion to AI"), firmly in the
operator's hands, closing on where your judgement must take over and when to bring in a second set of eyes.
