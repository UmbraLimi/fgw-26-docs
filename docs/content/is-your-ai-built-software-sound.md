## Take
Is your AI-built software *sound*? You can't tell by using it for a few minutes — the parts most likely to
fail are the ones you can't see. The guide teaches how to check: an intuition throughline, edge-case
framing, human-nature testing → automated checks, and knowing your limits (then growing past them).
