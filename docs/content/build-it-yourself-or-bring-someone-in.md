## 1. Take
The call isn't a feeling, it's a **test you can run on each part**: *do I understand how it works, well
enough to tell whether it's right and fix it?* × *what's the risk to me if it quietly goes wrong?* The
answers make a 2×2 — keep / own it / leave-or-learn / bring-someone-in-or-learn — and only one cell (a
**black box your business depends on**) forces a call. AI **raised the floor** (self-build goes much
further than you'd expect) but moved the hard parts to the **end** of the journey, where an explicit test
beats a gut call. The through-line is empowerment: a clear test is *more* freeing than "trust your unease,"
because it names *which* part and *why* — and the danger cell always forks **bring-someone-in vs learn-it**
(grow past the limit), so agency stays with the reader.
