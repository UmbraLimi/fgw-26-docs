# Launch lead — cross-pillar (LinkedIn)

**Angle:** the three questions every buyer building with AI runs into — is it sound? can I steer it? do I
build it myself or bring someone in? — now have three places to turn. Teaching-first, not a "look what I
made" announcement. The **lead of the launch series** (`[POSTING-PLAN]`): a cross-pillar launch, then one
dedicated post per pillar (Trust → Steering → Deciding) across the biweekly cadence.

## Draft (v1 — Conv 058, pre-POST-REVIEW)

> If you've built something with AI, you've probably felt both things at once.
>
> First comes the wonder. You described what you wanted, and real working software came back. That
> amazement is earned, not hype.
>
> Then the questions start. Is what I got actually sound, or does it just look right? It's almost what I
> meant, so how do I get it the rest of the way? And when I hit the part I can't figure out, do I keep
> going on my own or bring someone in?
>
> I've spent 40+ years building software, and those three questions are the ones that matter now. The
> tools keep changing. Knowing what to trust, how to direct the work, and when to get help does not.
>
> So I've written three short guides, one for each question:
> - Is your AI-built software sound?
> - How do you get AI to build what you actually meant?
> - Should you build it all yourself, or bring someone in to help?
>
> They're free, with no sign-up, and written for people building for their own needs rather than for a living.
>
> If you're somewhere in that middle, I hope they help.
>
