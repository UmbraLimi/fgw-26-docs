# Launch lead — cross-pillar (LinkedIn)

**Angle:** the three questions every buyer building with AI runs into — is it sound? can I steer it? do I
build it myself or bring someone in? — now have three places to turn. Teaching-first, not a "look what I
made" announcement. The **lead of the launch series** (`[POSTING-PLAN]`): a cross-pillar launch, then one
dedicated post per pillar (Trust → Steering → Deciding) across the biweekly cadence.

## Final copy (approved Conv 058)

If you've built custom software with AI, you've probably felt both the wonder and the doubt.

First comes the wonder. You described what you wanted, and real working software came back. That amazement you earned.

Then the doubts set in.
- Is what I got back actually sound, or does it just look right?
- I got a lot of what I meant to build, but how do I get it the rest of the way?
- When I hit a part I can't figure out, do I keep going on my own or bring someone in?

I've spent 40+ years building software for clients, and AI is the latest and most powerful tool I've picked up. I use it every day, and you should too. But, using AI to build software comes with real challenges, and every one of them is learnable.

So I've written three short guides, one for each question:

- Is your AI-built software sound?
- How do you get AI to build what you actually meant?
- Should you build it all yourself, or bring someone in to help?

They're written for people building for their own needs, like you, from my experience writing software businesses rely on, with AI and without it.

I hope they help.
