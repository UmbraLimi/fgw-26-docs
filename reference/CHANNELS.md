# Channels & funnel — each medium's job

> **What this is.** The *channel axis* of the content references: the funnel Fraser's leads move through,
> and the one job each medium does within it. Consulted for **all social-media and website content work**,
> alongside `PERSONA.md` (who he is / how he thinks) and `VOICE.md` (how the copy sounds). This file
> answers *where this goes and what the channel is for*; those answer *who's speaking* and *how it sounds*.
> A **living doc** — change log at the bottom. Created Conv 038 at the operator's direction (DEC at /r-end).
>
> **Sources.** Operator (direct, Conv 038 — funnel model, channel roles, secondary entry point) ·
> `PERSONA.md` (capacity, audience, posture).

## Two entry points (top of funnel)

Word of mouth alone is a lottery, not a pipeline — so the funnel has **two** entries, by different
mechanisms:

1. **Word of mouth — the engine (traditional, but unpredictable).** Someone vouches. The lever isn't a
   post — it's making referral easy and referrers look good via fresh **proof assets** (testimonials,
   LinkedIn recommendations, case records). *Optional future reinforcement (not yet adopted): cultivating
   a few referral partnerships — adjacent professionals who meet his buyers — to make this less random.*
2. **Inbound discovery — the cultivated second entry (search + GEO).** Intent-driven *pull*: a buyer with
   a live problem searches, or asks an AI assistant, and Fraser's evergreen teaching content answers them.
   Genuinely different from WoM — it reaches people with no prior connection — so it actually diversifies
   the top of funnel. The site's evergreen content *is* the acquisition asset (see Website, and the blog
   decision in `[POSTING-PLAN]`).

## The core reframe

**The social channels (LinkedIn, X) are a confirmation-and-qualification layer, not cold acquisition** —
people reach them already carrying Fraser's name. The **one deliberate exception** is the site's evergreen
content, which search and AI assistants turn into a true acquisition surface (entry point 2). Designing
LinkedIn or X as a cold-acquisition engine re-breaks the funnel.

Reinforced by capacity (`PERSONA.md`): **1–2 multi-month projects at a time** → ~1–2 good-fit buyers per
several months, not a lead stream. Low volume, high fit. Everything is for credibility and
*qualification* — attract the right fit, let the wrong fit self-select out — never volume.

## The flow

> **Referral path:** name heard → **LinkedIn** (credibility) → *how he thinks* → **X** (personality)
> → *when serious* → **Website** (qualify + contact)
>
> **Inbound path:** problem in hand → search / AI assistant → **Website** evergreen content
> → (may glance at LinkedIn/X to confirm the person) → **Website** (contact)

Both converge on the site. Direction matters: every channel points *toward the site* once intent turns
serious. The site is the only place with the real contact mechanism; platform DMs stay a light touch.

## Word of mouth — the engine

- **The traditional lead source** — not something a post moves directly; unpredictable on its own, hence
  entry point 2 exists to complement it.
- **The lever:** fresh proof assets + easy referral. Higher-leverage than any single post.

## LinkedIn — positioning & credibility (the look-you-up moment)

- **One job:** the professional credibility check when Fraser's name gets passed along — who he is, how he
  works, the proof, visibly *alive and thinking*.
- **Register:** positioning / approach. The buyer audience lives here (`PERSONA.md`).
- **Cadence:** **biweekly (~2/month)** pillar posts — enough to read *alive and thinking* to a buyer
  checking the profile, without a treadmill. Sustainability over reach: a modest cadence held beats an
  ambitious one dropped (a burst then silence reads as abandoned). *Not* asked to generate cold leads.
- **Links:** out to the site (serious contact) and to X (more of how he thinks).

## X — personality & point of view (the warming moment)

- **One job:** the human behind the positioning — personality, perspective, a fuller sense of how he
  thinks.
- **Posture — downstream of LinkedIn.** Seen mostly by people who already found him and want more; **not**
  a cold-acquisition channel aimed at buyers (not densely on X anyway). It *supports* LinkedIn.
- **Cadence:** opportunistic — an adapted post only when a LinkedIn piece has a personable angle; X keeps
  no schedule of its own.
- **Avoid:** reactive news-chasing — the highest-maintenance format, first to lapse at his bandwidth.
  Personality travels without a news peg.
- **Links:** out to the site; cross-referenced from LinkedIn.
- **No X Premium (decided Conv 038):** X stays lean/free — its job needs neither paid reach (would amplify
  into the builder crowd, not buyers) nor long posts (long-form lives on the blog; X links to it). Revisit
  only if X's role is ever upgraded.
- **Link cards don't render on X (observed Conv 038).** X no longer shows image preview cards for external
  links; a linked post appears as text + a bare link. For a visual, attach an image as media (there's no
  card to suppress) — the Conv-038 pin attached the branded `og-image.jpg` this way.

## Website — qualification & conversion (and the inbound acquisition surface)

- **One job at the serious moment:** the only place with the real contact mechanism and the
  self-qualification path (straw/tin/steel phases, `/phases`). All arrows point here.
- **Doubles as entry point 2 — the content home (decided Conv 038): a guides + blog hub-and-spoke.**
  - **Guides (cornerstone, *undated*):** ~3 deep pillar pages (Trust / Steering / Deciding) — the GEO/SEO
    backbone search and AI assistants surface. Timeless-reference framing, each its own indexable URL, no
    staleness signal. The acquisition assets.
  - **Blog (dated posts):** shorter observations / de-identified project stories / Judgement-thread pieces
    — the *alive and thinking* stream. Each links *up* to its cornerstone guide; a guide may list related
    posts. A stream is free to have a natural rhythm because the undated guides carry the timeless weight.
  - **Write-once, distribute:** the blog post is the **owned home**; LinkedIn gets an adapted version, X
    the personable ones. The biweekly cadence feeds the **blog first, socials second** — the durable,
    indexable copy lives on the site, not the platforms (this is what solves social ephemerality).
  - **Build** is an `fgw-26` board row (dovetails with `[SITE-STRUCTURED-DATA]` for GEO markup).

## Topic pillars — what every channel draws from

Three **buyer-problem-facing** pillars (each a question a real buyer asks — so each doubles as an evergreen
inbound topic), plus one cross-cutting thread. Everything on LinkedIn, X, and the site pulls from this set,
so the channels stay consistent. Grounds in `PERSONA.md` ("what he educates about").

1. **Trust — is this AI-built software sound?** What AI gets confidently wrong, how to judge whether what
   you got will hold, how to spot the gaps. Answers the buyer's real question ("will I get burned?") and
   catches the straw-man searcher. The primary inbound-discovery magnet.
2. **Steering — becoming a worthy companion to AI.** The human skill of directing it: prompts, context,
   closing the last-mile gap so it builds what you actually mean. Catches the tin-man keeping AI on course.
3. **Deciding — DIY vs. bring someone in.** Where you are on the straw/tin/steel arc, what's worth keeping
   vs. starting over, when self-build ends and you call someone (the home-repair analogy). Sets up the soft
   CTA naturally.

**Cross-cutting thread — Judgement from 40+ years.** Not a pillar but a flavour woven through all three,
and the heart of X's personality: grounded stories, candid misses, the throughline that the tool was never
the interesting question. Supplies credibility by proof and the human voice; seasoning surfaces quietly
(`PERSONA.md` / `VOICE.md`).

## Sourcing & workflow — where posts come from, how they're made

**Sourcing — the well the pillars draw from:**
- **Capture habit** — a running *seeds* backlog (the notebook-handy propensity): client questions, moments
  from live work, angles, jotted as they arise. Cheapest, highest-yield source.
- **Mine the case records** — already-written, already mask-cleared material; repurpose pillar-shaped
  lessons at low effort.
- **Live-project drip** — a few *de-identified* observations over a project's months; the
  `[CASE-RECORD-SIGNOFF]` masking guard applies.
- **40-years stories** — feed the Judgement thread.
- **Not** reactive AI-news (see X → Avoid).

**Workflow — CC drafts, the operator owns the voice:**
- CC turns a captured seed + the pillars into a **candidate post**; the operator redlines it to final (his
  voice, his name); then publish. Batch 2–3 per sitting to fit the biweekly rhythm.
- 🔴 **Posts and site content ship only with operator approval.** CC drafts and makes every edit, but
  post and site/deliverable copy goes live only after the operator approves it (`DEC-024-1` / `DEC-037-6`):
  CC drafts → operator approves → publish.
- **Backlog:** a lightweight durable list — seeds → drafts → published — kept at `_cc/content-backlog.md`
  (alt: a board section; chose a topic note to keep the board for commitments).

## Social copy conventions (LinkedIn + X, not the site)

- 🔴 **Em-dashes: surfaced for review, not banned (revised Conv 038).** Before publishing *any* new content
  (site page, blog, guide, or a LinkedIn/X post), CC surfaces every em-dash for the operator to rule — the
  all-content em-dash sanity check in `CLAUDE.md`. Already-vetted existing copy stands. (Supersedes the
  earlier Conv-038 "no em-dashes on LinkedIn/X"; the operator had already vetted the live LinkedIn/X/site
  copy, so a retroactive sweep would be false positives.)
- 🔴 **Prefer complete sentences on LinkedIn or X.** Sentence fragments read as a punchy, promotional
  cadence (ad copy, not a seasoned person talking) and are themselves an AI-marketing tell. One rhetorical
  question is fine; a pile-up of fragments is not. (Operator directive, Conv 038.)

## Proof assets — shared fuel (cross-channel)

Testimonials, LinkedIn recommendations, and case records feed word of mouth and LinkedIn alike. Keep them
current — the credibility currency the whole funnel spends.

## Measurement — qualitative by necessity

With word of mouth and intent-driven discovery at the top, leads resist attribution — a serious person
arrives already convinced. Success is **qualitative**: inbound that already fits and mentions the profile,
the site, or a specific piece. Don't chase vanity metrics (counter to `PERSONA.md`).
